### Explanation: Median vs N50 Difference:

Median read lengths are defined as the unweighted middle value of all individual read lengths, while the N50 is the length-weighted median statistic. The N50 is the sequence length of the shortest contig at 50% of the total assembly
length. The median indicates that 50% of reads are smaller values, while the other 50% are greater values. The N50 indicates how long contiguous sequences are and the overall quality of an assembly. For our particular methods section 
and reporting, the N50 is a more valuable measure than the median, as our goal focuses on finding the fraction of reads that span a complete transcript. The N50 already indicates that the assembly is likely to be of decent quality 
when compared to the median statistic.
